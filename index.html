Where rifts spawn

Each opening, the portal appears at one of a fixed set of spots, and which spot is picked changes every time. When the chat notice appears, open your world map, zoom into the highlighted region and look for the purple portal icon. Wait near a teleport point by the clusters below so you can reach it inside the 10-minute window.
